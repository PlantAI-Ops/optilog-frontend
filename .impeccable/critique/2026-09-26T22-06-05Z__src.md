**Cognitive load: 3 of 8 checklist failures → moderate.** Fails: *single focus* (dashboard: 6 competing regions), *one thing at a time* (end-shift combines handover + maintenance scheduling + approval notice), *minimal choices* (Type filter = 8 chips, Source = 7, console nav = 10, status picker = 5). Decision points with >4 visible options: `events.tsx:14-15`, `ConsoleShell.tsx:20-32`, `EventEditor.tsx:5`.

**Emotional journey:** The peak is real — the record puck, haptics, live waveform and transcript preview at `index.tsx:667-712` is the product's one expressive moment and it earns it. The valleys are all silent failures: a transcription that quietly returns an empty draft, a mic button that invents 15 minutes, a "Dictate note" that writes someone else's sentence. Per the peak-end rule the ending is the weak point — the last screen of the shift (`report.tsx`) is a dead Share button. Reassurance at high-stakes moments is good: "Not yet — back to shift" and the approval pill both do their job.

## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment: Authored, with a leak at the console.** The floor world could not be swapped onto another product — the 224px red puck, weight-900 headline stack, uppercase micro-labels over 56-80px controls, and the amber/red semantic ration are all drawn from this product's operating conditions. The audit distinction (Observation / Reported cause / Suspected cause / Verified cause / Action taken as separate `CardLine`s at `index.tsx:602-614`, `timeline.tsx:128-134`, `events.tsx:127-130`) is executed with unusual discipline and is the strongest argument the interface is authored for OptiLog specifically. The console is where it leaks: stat-card row → 2-column table/list → 2-column table/list is the default shadcn admin composition, and could carry inventory, HR, or CRM data unchanged.

**Deterministic scan:** 26 findings, all `advisory`, exit code 2 — 23 `design-system-font-size`, 2 `design-system-color`, 1 `design-system-radius`. Concentrated in `PlantSetupForm.tsx` (15 × literal `10px`), plus `ConsoleShell.tsx:183` (11px SourceBadge), `console/calendar.tsx:111,273` (8px, 11px), `ui/form.tsx:131,153` and `ui/calendar.tsx:79,85` (0.8rem), and `lib/error-page.ts` (hex `#4b5563` / `#d1d5db`, `0.375rem` radius, `1.25rem`).

- Detector caught what the review under-weighted: sub-ramp sizes in console chrome.
- False positives / design-system self-conflict: `ConsoleShell.tsx:183` is flagged, yet DESIGN.md's own Chips spec documents SourceBadge as "11px weight 600" — the ramp is missing the step the component spec requires. `ui/form.tsx` and `ui/calendar.tsx` are shadcn vendor primitives; editing them forks the library.
- Detector could not see (runtime/semantic): the undefined `info` token, dead buttons, missing dialog semantics, absent auth guard, color-only status.

**Visual overlays:** none — no browser automation tool exposed in this session, so no tab, no script injection, no user-visible overlay.

## Overall Impression

The floor flow is a genuinely good piece of product design undermined by a handful of places where the interface misreports reality — and in an audit product, misreporting is the one defect that damages the thing you're selling. The console is competent, dense and honest about data, but has no voice of its own and no help for the person who has to live in it. The single biggest opportunity: make every status in the product tell the truth about the system, then give the console a point of view.

## What's Working

1. **The record puck and its feedback stack** (`index.tsx:667-712`). Level-driven glow, pulse ring, 13-bar waveform, haptic pattern, live transcript — one expressive moment at exactly the right amplitude.
2. **The audit distinction is never collapsed.** Observation, Reported, Suspected, Verified and Action taken stay as separately-labelled blocks on the confirm card, the timeline detail, the events expander and the editor — four places, no shortcuts.
3. **The two-button footer.** `flex-1` neutral / `flex-[2]` amber at 56px encodes which action is real through geometry rather than colour alone.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The floor misreports what the operator did — three times.**
- Why it matters: `index.tsx:540-547` — the clarify step's `aria-label="Answer by voice"` button hardcodes `setClarifyValue("15")`. `end-shift.tsx:103-111` — "Dictate note" with a Mic icon injects a canned sentence into a handover note. `index.tsx:397-406` — transcription failure falls through to an empty draft with only a `console.error`; the operator taps Confirm and commits an event whose observation is blank. Each violates Product Principle #1 while appearing to satisfy it.
- Fix: Make the mic button genuinely capture or remove it; same for Dictate note. On transcription failure, show an explicit banner — "Speech wasn't transcribed — type the observation" — and block Confirm until observation or transcript is non-empty.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P1] Modal dialogs are keyboard traps, and status is color-only.**
- Why it matters: `timeline.tsx:302` and `console/admin/index.tsx:101` are bare `div.fixed inset-0` overlays with no `role="dialog"`, no `aria-modal`, no Escape, no focus trap — while Radix `Dialog` sits unused at `components/ui/dialog.tsx`. `timeline.tsx:35-40` renders confirmed / investigating / draft as the same `CircleDot` differing only by hue — color-only status, failing WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.1.
- Fix: Replace both hand-rolled modals with the in-repo Dialog/AlertDialog primitives. Give each status a distinct glyph, not just a distinct hue.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable audit`

**[P1] Every authenticated surface is reachable unauthenticated, with demo credentials prefilled.**
- Why it matters: `__root.tsx:141-145` renders `<Outlet />` unconditionally and `console/route.tsx` has no `beforeLoad` guard — `/console` shows plant "—" with six failing queries; `/timeline` shows "No events yet. Tap RECORD" to someone who cannot record. `index.tsx:49-50` prefills `admin@optilog.com` / `demo1234`.
- Fix: Add `beforeLoad` redirects on console and shift route groups; clear prefilled credentials behind an env flag.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P2] Console status signals are decorative, and a floor status banner renders invisible.**
- Why it matters: "Data layer live" (`ConsoleShell.tsx:100-102`) is a static green dot, breaking the Signal-Only Rule. Error banners append raw `error.message` with no retry (`console/index.tsx:84-92`). Teams / Open investigations / Worst assets render blank when empty. `index.tsx:208-209` uses `border-info/40 bg-info/10 text-info`, tokens DESIGN.md says don't exist — the banner loses its tint entirely.
- Fix: Derive the live dot from real connectivity (`state.online`), add "Try again" to error banners, write empty states, swap `info` for `warning` or `primary`.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable audit`

**[P2] Console cognitive overload: 10 nav items, 15 filter chips, 6 dashboard regions.**
- Why it matters: The rail (`ConsoleShell.tsx:20-32`) lists nine destinations plus Admin before content. The events page offers 8 Type + 7 Source chips simultaneously (`events.tsx:14-15`) with no search, no date range, no URL persistence — Back loses your filters. The dashboard stacks four stats, a performance table, a team list, an event list, an investigation list and a worst-assets list in one screen.
- Fix: Collapse related destinations, demote Type/Source to searchable selects, persist filters in the URL, collapse secondary dashboard lists behind "Show more".
- Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User) — console supervisor**
- No keyboard shortcuts, no search on Events, and `events.tsx:45` hardcodes `todayStr()` — finding yesterday's breakdown is impossible; the core loop is not completable.
- Table expansion is `onClick` on a `<tr>` (`events.tsx:101-104`) with no `tabIndex`, `aria-expanded`, or key handler. Keyboard path to event detail: none.
- Filters live in component state, not the URL — Back and refresh both discard them.
- Ten-item rail with no command palette or quick-jump.

**Sam (Accessibility-dependent) — keyboard/screen reader**

- Status conveyed by color alone at `timeline.tsx:35-40` — confirmed and investigating are the same shape in different hues.
- `console/admin/index.tsx:101` repeats the same trap.
